Exceptional Historic Wealth UNESCO World Heritage Sites: Baalbek houses some of the most colossal and well-preserved Roman temples on Earth. Byblos, the birthplace of the linear alphabet, has been continuously inhabited for over 7,000 years. Multimillennial Layers: Ruins from Phoenician, Roman, Umayyad (Anjar), and Crusader eras stand side by side across the country. Holy Qadisha Valley: A historic sanctuary carved into dramatic cliffs, home to early Christian monasteries and hermitages. Biodiversity & Natural Wonders Ancient Cedar Reserves: The Shouf Cedar Biosphere Reserve and the Cedars of God forest shelter centuries-old trees and protected mountain wildlife. Spectacular Geology: The Jeita Grotto features towering limestone formations, while the Baatara Gorge waterfall drops through three natural rock bridges. Coastal Ecosystems: The Tyre Coast Nature Reserve features pristine sand dunes critical for sea turtle nesting.
